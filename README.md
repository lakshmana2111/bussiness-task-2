### Business Insights

**Technology is the most profitable category**
Our analysis revealed that **Technology** products generate the highest total profit, significantly outperforming other categories. Conversely, some categories, such as **Furniture**, are experiencing substantial losses.

**Discounts negatively impact profit**
There is a clear **negative correlation between Discount and Profit**. Higher discounts generally lead to lower profits, and in many cases, can significantly reduce or even result in negative profit.
